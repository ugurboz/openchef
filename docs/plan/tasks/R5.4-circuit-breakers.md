## Goal

The debate of R5.1–R5.3 stays bounded however the agents behave and whatever quota is
left: a step that stops making progress is escalated once and then handed to the user;
debate turns pass quota admission and fit one task-wide budget; a pool under pressure
lowers the debate depth. The depth table and the budget are configurable in
`[debate]`.

## Design (D-040)

### Stagnation (pipeline tasks)

1. **Signals**, kept for one step run (a failover or a daemon restart starts over):
   - the same verification failure twice in a row: the fingerprint of the exit status
     and the normalized output (ANSI stripped, digit runs as `0`, the worktree path as
     `<worktree>`, whitespace collapsed), after an implement turn or a review fix;
   - oscillation: after each turn of the implementer the worktree's state (a git tree
     of every change, `.openchef` excluded) equals a state before the previous one;
   - a reopened finding: a blocker or major with the id and file of a finding settled
     `fixed` earlier in the step.
2. **Escalation:** the implementer's attempt ends `failed` ("stagnated: …"). One new
   attempt (role `implement`) goes to a frontier agent (routed as `critical`) on a pool
   other than the implementer's; its one turn gets the step, what stagnated and the
   evidence, on the worktree as the implementer left it. Verification runs once, without
   retries: passing → the step is committed (the escalation is the last word: no
   further review; its `FIXED` claims are taken, other findings stay open); anything
   else → the step fails and the task needs attention, the reason naming both.
3. **No escalation** (a pinned task, no such agent, the budget used up) → the step
   fails and the task needs attention at once.
4. `run` tasks keep §7.2 unchanged: their retries are already bounded and there is no
   debate to break.

### Quota

5. Reviewers, critics and arbiters are routed through admission (R5.3). When
   independent agents exist but admission or a cooldown refuses them all, the review,
   council or arbitration is recorded as **skipped for quota**; a judging turn that
   fails for quota is too, and its pool cools down.
6. A fix turn (review fix, upheld fix) that fails for quota or a rate limit is an
   implement turn's failure (§7.2): the work is saved as a patch, the step fails over,
   and the task waits for quota when no pool is left.
7. Review rounds continue across attempts: a step's review starts after its last
   recorded round, with that round's open findings, so a failover or a restart never
   repeats rounds already paid for and the round limit holds per step.

### Budget

8. `[debate] max_turns_per_task` (default 2 × steps + 4; at the council, the plan's
   steps) caps critic, revision, review, arbiter, fix and escalation turns of a task
   together, repair turns included. Planning, implement turns and §7.2 retries do not
   count.
9. The count lives on the task (migration 0006) and is spent before each turn with one
   conditional update, so a restart never refills it. Used up → no review, council
   round or arbitration starts, no fix turn runs (its findings stay open), stagnation
   needs attention; a step still commits when its verification passes. One notice per
   run says so.

### Depth

10. `[debate.<importance>]` sets `council_critics` (0–3), `council_rounds` (0–5) and
    `review_rounds` (0–5); defaults are the §7.5 table. Critics without rounds (or the
    reverse) is a configuration error.
11. A pool with `effort_profile = "auto"` in `yellow` or `red` health lowers the depth
    by one importance level, at most once per debate: checked on the planner's or
    implementer's pool before the debate and on each judge's pool when it is routed.
    A notice records it; nothing raises the depth.

## Scope

- `crates/openchef-engine` — `breakers` module (fingerprint, progress, escalation),
  `debate` module (depth table, budget), engine `Config::debate`, event `stagnated`,
  `Router::pressure`, `NoAgent::quota`
- `crates/openchef-gitws` — `Worktree::state`
- `crates/openchef-store` — migration 0006 (`tasks.debate_turns`), spend and read
- `crates/openchef-config` — `[debate]`
- `crates/openchef-daemon` — `[debate]` into the engine, pressure from quota health
- `crates/openchef-api`, `crates/openchef-cli` — debate turns in `status`, the
  `stagnated` event in the live renderer

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The same verification failure twice → one escalation turn on another pool →
      verified → committed by the escalation agent.
- [ ] Without an escalation agent the step needs attention after the second identical
      failure, not after the retries.
- [ ] Oscillation and a reopened finding are detected (unit and service tests).
- [ ] A reviewer refused by quota is recorded as skipped for quota.
- [ ] A fix turn out of quota fails over; the next attempt's review continues the
      rounds.
- [ ] `max_turns_per_task` reached → no further debate turn, the step still commits.
- [ ] A pressured `auto` pool lowers the depth; `[debate]` parses, validates and
      round-trips.
