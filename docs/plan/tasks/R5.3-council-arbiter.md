# R5.3 — Plan council, arbiter, disputed findings

## Goal

For `high` and `critical` tasks, critics of other providers read the plan before the
user approves it; the planner revises it in its own session; whatever the critics still
find is shown next to the plan at approval. During a step's review, findings the
implementer rebuts go to one arbiter on a third pool, whose verdict either dismisses them
or sends them back for one last fix. Every turn stays bounded and frugal (D-029).

## Design (D-039)

### Plan council

1. **Depth** from the task's importance after planning (the `--importance` floor, else
   the highest step importance the planner proposed): `low`/`normal` → none; `high` →
   1 critic, at most 2 critique rounds; `critical` → 2 critics on two other providers
   when available (else 1), at most 3 critique rounds.
2. **Rounds are critique rounds.** Round *n*: every critic answers with findings; no
   blocker or major → the plan is locked; otherwise the planner revises and round
   *n+1* critiques the revision. The last round's findings are not revised: they are
   shown at approval, where the user is the arbiter. (Review rounds of D-038 are fix
   rounds because no person checks code before the commit; a plan always has one.)
3. **Critics:** attempts with role `council` at step 0, routed among agents whose
   runtime differs from the planner's (and from each other's), `plan` mode, structured
   output. A critic keeps its session across rounds, so it reads the repository once
   and sees what changed. Prompt: the task, the plan JSON, its earlier findings.
4. **Located plan findings:** a blocker or major names a file of the repository or a
   step (`contract: "step 2"`, within the plan); anything else is a nit. Same findings
   schema and validation as D-038.
5. **Revision:** the planner's session stays open through the council. The revision
   turn carries the blocking findings and asks for the whole revised plan, validated
   like the first (one repair turn). There is no rebuttal channel: the next critique
   decides whether a finding still stands.
6. **Never blocking:** a critic that fails or answers invalidly twice is dropped for the
   rest of the council (recorded); with no critic left the plan goes to approval as is.
   A revision invalid after its repair keeps the last valid plan, with a notice.
7. **Records:** council findings in `findings` at step 0, per round; a finding the next
   round does not report again is `fixed`; the last round's blocking ones stay `open`.
   `openchef plan <task>`, the `pipeline` output and `status <task>` list the open ones
   next to the plan; `--yes` still approves (the findings stay in the report).
8. **Recovery:** the council runs while the task is `planning`; a daemon restart
   restarts planning (D-037) and ends running council attempts as interrupted.
9. **Side by side:** two critics of one task run at once, so the store keeps one
   running attempt per critic for role `council` (migration 0005, index only); every
   other role keeps one running attempt per step.

### Arbiter

10. **When:** once per step, after its review loop ends (D-038), if any blocker or major
   is `disputed`. One batched turn for all of them, never one per finding.
11. **Who:** role `arbiter` at the step, routed to a pool used by neither the
    implementer nor the step's reviewers, preferring a third provider; none available
    → the findings stay `disputed` (D-038 behaviour), with a notice.
12. **Input/output:** the findings with their evidence, the rebuttals, the step's
    bounded diff; `plan` mode; structured output
    `{"verdicts": [{"id", "verdict": "uphold" | "dismiss", "reason"}]}`, strict, one
    repair turn; still invalid → findings stay `disputed`.
13. **Effect:** `dismiss` → `dismissed`, the reason in `note`. `uphold` → `open`, then
    exactly one fix turn in the implementer's session for all upheld findings, with
    rebuttals no longer accepted; re-verification (§7.2); the implementer's `FIXED`
    claims are taken (no review is left to confirm them); the rest stay `open`.
14. **Cost bound per step:** ≤ 1 arbiter turn (+1 repair) + 1 fix turn, on top of
    D-038's bound. Quota admission and the task budget come with R5.4.

## Scope

- `crates/openchef-engine` — `council` module (prompts, revision, depth), arbiter in
  `reviewing.rs` + verdict schema in `review.rs`, planning keeps the planner session
  through the council, events `council_started` / `council_finished`,
  `arbitration_started` / `arbitration_finished`
- `crates/openchef-engine/src/router.rs`, `crates/openchef-daemon/src/router.rs` —
  `route_reviewer` becomes `route_independent` (runtimes and pools to avoid), used by
  reviewers, critics and arbiters with the `review` phase's routing
- `crates/openchef-store` — settle `disputed` findings (verdicts); migration 0005
- `crates/openchef-api`, `crates/openchef-cli` — open council findings in `plan`,
  `pipeline` and `status`; the live renderer

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `high` task: one critic of another provider, blocker → planner revises in the same
      session → second critique clean → the plan awaits approval with no open findings.
- [ ] `critical` task with two other providers: two critics; the round limit leaves the
      last findings `open` and `openchef plan` shows them next to the plan.
- [ ] `normal` task: no council turn.
- [ ] A failing critic and an invalid revision never block approval (recorded).
- [ ] A disputed blocker with a third pool: one arbiter turn; `dismiss` → `dismissed`;
      `uphold` → one fix turn, re-verification, `fixed` on a `FIXED` claim.
- [ ] No third pool: findings stay `disputed`, a notice says why.
- [ ] Invalid verdicts: one repair turn, then the findings stay `disputed`.
