# R5.2 — Findings schema, reviewer, review → fix loop

| Owner | Milestone | Depends on | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | R5 | R5.1 merged | L |

## Goal

After a step passes verification, a reviewer from a **different provider** reads the
step's diff in `plan` mode and answers with schema-validated findings. Blockers and
majors open a fix turn in the implementer's still-open session; the implementer fixes
or rebuts each one; every fix is re-verified before the next round; the step is
committed when no blocker or major is open or the round limit is hit. Nits and
unresolved findings are kept for the report. Plan council and arbiters come in R5.3,
stagnation and budget breakers in R5.4.

## Design (D-038)

1. **Findings document.** `{"findings": [{id, severity: blocker|major|nit, file?,
   line?, contract?, claim, evidence, fix}]}`. Validation is strict like the plan's.
   A blocker or major with neither a file nor a contract is downgraded to a nit
   (§7.5): only located defects cost a turn. Ids are unique within a review.
2. **Depth.** From the step's importance (the task's importance is the floor):
   `low` → no review; `normal` → 1 round; `high` → 2; `critical` → 3, frontier tier.
   `[debate]` configuration and quota-lowered depth come with R5.4.
3. **Reviewer.** An attempt with role `review` at the step, routed among agents whose
   runtime differs from the implementer's (another provider); none available → the
   review is skipped and recorded, never done by the same provider. One session per
   round (fresh eyes), `plan` mode, structured output. The prompt carries the step's
   goal, its verification command and the diff since the last verified commit
   (tracked changes and untracked files, capped at 60 000 characters with a note when
   cut), plus the open findings of earlier rounds.
4. **Fix turn.** In the implementer's open session: the blockers and majors with their
   evidence and suggested fix. The implementer answers per finding with `FIXED <id>` or
   `REBUT <id>: <evidence>` lines; a rebuttal marks the finding `disputed` (the arbiter
   of R5.3 settles it; until then it costs no further turn). The fix is re-verified; a
   failing verification follows §7.2's retry rules.
5. **Commit.** When no blocker/major is open, or the rounds are used up, the step is
   committed as today. Open findings at that point stay `open` and are highlighted in
   the report.
6. **Records.** A `findings` table: task, step, round, reviewer attempt, id, severity,
   file, line, contract, claim, evidence, fix, status (`open`, `fixed`, `disputed`,
   `nit`), so the report (R7) and `openchef status <task>` can show them. Events
   `review_started` / `review_finished` carry the counts for the live view.
7. **Recovery.** A daemon restart during a review ends the review attempt
   (interrupted); the step resumes as today (its implementer session resumes), and the
   review runs again after the next verification.

## Scope

- `crates/openchef-engine` — `review` module (document, schema, prompts), the loop in
  `step.rs`, events
- `crates/openchef-store` — migration 0004 `findings`, its repository
- `crates/openchef-gitws` — the step diff
- `crates/openchef-engine/src/router.rs`, `crates/openchef-daemon/src/router.rs` — routing
  a reviewer away from the implementer's provider and to a minimum tier
- `crates/openchef-cli` — findings in `status <task>` and the live renderer

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A normal-importance step with a blocker: one fix turn, re-verification, commit; the
      finding is `fixed`; the reviewer ran on the other provider in `plan` mode.
- [ ] A rebuttal marks the finding `disputed` and costs no further turn.
- [ ] Unlocated blockers are downgraded to nits; nits never cost a turn.
- [ ] The round limit commits with the remaining findings `open`.
- [ ] `low` importance skips review; no other provider available skips it with a notice.
- [ ] Invalid reviewer output: one repair turn, then the review is skipped (recorded),
      the step is not blocked.
